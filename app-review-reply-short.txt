3. FUNCTION AND AUDIENCE
Trendzo Retailer is a B2B tool for registered apparel retail businesses in India. Not a consumer app: no public storefront, and every account is manually approved before features unlock.
Small retailers cannot afford studio photography per garment, yet need quality imagery to sell online. The retailer photographs a garment on their phone; our backend generates studio-quality product and on-model images, published as catalog products with variants, pricing and inventory. Also: business KYC, store online/offline control, read-only earnings view.

Account closure: Profile > Request account closure, in-app. Confirming closes the account and suspends the store: sign-in ends, catalog and media leave active access, personal details are anonymized. As every account is a GST-registered business, Indian tax law requires retaining invoices, orders and payout records for a statutory period; these are kept only that long. https://api.trendzonow.com/account-deletion
